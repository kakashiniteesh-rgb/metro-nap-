Metro Nap

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

In short: Metro Nap has no account, no server and no ads. Your location and your alarm stops stay on your phone. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

What the app does with your location

Metro Nap uses your phone's location to work out which Delhi Metro station is nearest to you and how far you are from the stops you chose, so it can wake you before your stop. When an alarm is switched on, location keeps working with the screen off, and Android shows a notification while this is happening. Your location is processed on your phone only and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What is stored on your phone

  • The stops you add (name and place), your alarm sound and distance choices.
  • Your settings, theme and card layout.

This stays in the app's private storage on your phone. Android backups are switched off for the app. You can remove everything by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Permissions and why

  • Location (including "all the time"): to track your trip and wake you at the right stop.
  • Notifications, full-screen alerts and display over other apps: to show the alarm clearly, even on the lock screen or over other apps.
  • Vibration, wake lock and exact alarms: to make the alarm reliable.

Network use

Metro Nap works without an internet connection. When you are online it may load its text font from Google Fonts, which means Google can see your device's IP address. If you tap "Open in Maps" on a station, the station's coordinates are sent to Google Maps in your browser. Metro Nap does not send your own location anywhere.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
